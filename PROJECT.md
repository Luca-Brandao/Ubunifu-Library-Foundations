# Coursework context

Ubunifu Library: Foundations was developed for **OOP152 SS2** as part of my Bachelor of Business Information Systems at STADIO Higher Education.

This portfolio edition uses fictional member and staff identities and example.com contact addresses. Book authors and the historical figures in quiz questions are reference content, not sample user records. Notebook outputs, submitted form data, compiled files, original assignment PDFs and the presentation video are excluded. Minor runtime and portability fixes are documented in the README.
